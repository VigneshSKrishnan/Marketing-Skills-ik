Short: ImageKit is a media lifecycle platform for websites and apps, combining Agentic DAM, creative automation, and Image & Video APIs to help teams manage assets, create on-brand creatives, transform and optimize media, and deliver images and videos at scale.

Long: ImageKit is a media lifecycle platform for websites and apps: one system for managing assets, producing on-brand creative, and delivering images and video to production, without an export or a handoff in between. It works with the storage you already have, including S3, GCS, and your own server, and with a built-in Agentic DAM for the assets you want findable, governed, and operable by agents, tagged by LLMs in your business's own vocabulary rather than generic labels. Creative teams generate on-brand variants from templates, with GenAI where they need it. Developers get 50+ real-time transformations and adaptive streaming that works with any player. And through ImageKit's MCP servers, an agent can do the work end to end, from finding the approved assets and resizing them for every screen to publishing them live, under the same permissions as the person who asked. 300,000+ developers and 3,000 businesses in 85 countries run on ImageKit, with enterprise-grade reliability and without enterprise pricing.
